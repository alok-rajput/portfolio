About Me

I am Alok Rajput from Kannauj, Uttar Pradesh. I completed my basic education in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, and graduated in Electrical Engineering from NIT Silchar in 2026.

My interests lie in Signal Processing, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Computer Vision, with a focus on building practical engineering solutions. During my academic journey, I explored both core electrical engineering systems and AI-driven technologies through research and project development. I interned at Panki Thermal Power Plant, Kanpur, where I gained exposure to industrial power systems and electrical operations. My curiosity toward AI and intelligent systems also led me to work on signal and audio processing projects with researchers from IIT Delhi and IIT Guwahati.

For my final year thesis titled “Studies on Underwater Image Enhancement and Object Detection Techniques for Maritime Surveillance”, I worked on underwater image enhancement and AI-based object detection for maritime applications. The work resulted in both conference and journal publications.

I enjoy combining engineering fundamentals with computational intelligence to develop impactful real-world solutions.
