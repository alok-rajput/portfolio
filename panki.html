Panki Thermal Power Plant Internship

Completed industrial training at 660 MW Panki Thermal Power Plant, Kanpur, where I gained practical exposure to large scale power generation and transmission systems.

  • Studied steam turbine based electricity generation and the complete thermal power plant operation cycle including boiler, turbine, generator and condenser systems.
  • Analyzed switchyard components such as circuit breakers, busbars, isolators, transformers and protection systems used in high-voltage power transmission.
  • Worked on ETAP Grid based transmission modeling and performed load flow analysis to understand system stability and power flow behavior.
  • Observed real industrial practices related to power generation, monitoring, safety and electrical maintenance.
